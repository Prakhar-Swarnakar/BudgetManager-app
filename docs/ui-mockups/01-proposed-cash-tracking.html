Cash tracking — mockup for review

Know how much cash you're physically holding. You manually log cash coming in (an ATM withdrawal, a cash gift — a "top-up"); a cash spend already works today via Add Transaction's existing Payment method = Cash, no change needed there. The balance is never a number stored anywhere - it's derived live, same way every other total in this app already works.

  • New side-panel page, not a 5th bottom tab - sits with Monthly budget / Category rules / Taxonomy rules, reached from the menu, same as those.
  • Balance = sum of top-ups − sum of cash spends (the full transaction amount, not the budget-counted share - if you pay a split bill in cash, the whole amount leaves your hand even though friends owe their part back some other way).
  • A negative balance is a signal, not a normal state - it means a top-up wasn't logged, or something got mis-tagged as Cash. Shown in red with an explicit note, not treated like Home's "Saved" (which legitimately can go negative).
  • Open question for you: should a cash spend row link to its category (tap → Category detail), same as every other amount/category row in the app? Mocked here as yes - costs nothing, keeps it traceable.
