Checked against MessagesViewModel. The first one matters most, because the new Undo button would make an existing risk easy to trigger.

What changed in this pass

Your decisions, applied

From your answers to the first mockup.

Opens on To review

“Not assigned” is renamed “To review” everywhere, chip first, default when its count is above zero.

Swipe reveals a button

No more inline buttons on the row and no auto-decide on swipe. The row slides open, the button waits about 5 seconds. Needs a reveal-and-hold SwipeRow instead of today's SwipeToDismissBox threshold.

Confirm, Reject, Undo

“Accepted” becomes “Confirmed”. Green for Confirm, red for Reject, grey for Undo, on swipe buttons, sheet buttons and status chips.

+ and − for direction

“−₹486” for money out, “+₹12,000” in green for money in. Arrows removed.

Removed

Progress bar under the month and “Accept all with a suggestion”.

Kept

Overflow menu for re-scan, re-apply rules and delete month; status badge on the avatar in All; active filter chips; All caught up.

Missed in the first pass

Things the app already does that the first mockup dropped or got wrong.

Split amounts

Your share is the main amount with the full amount struck through under it, plus “Split with 2 friends” on the row and “your share” plus who owes what in the sheet. Matches amountText and splitTotalText today.

Messages with no amount

The row shows “No amount” instead of a figure; confirming opens Add Transaction to fill it in.

Money in skips the form

Confirming a credit marks it Confirmed straight away, as the view model does today, so the row says “Money in, no form needed”.

Renamed titles

A confirmed message shows the transaction's own title (“Dinner at Toit”), not the merchant from the SMS.

Undo snackbar after reject

Still there, bottom of the list: “Rejected Zomato · Undo”.

Empty states

Empty month and filters-hide-everything now have their own wording and actions.

Detail sheet for decided messages

Confirmed and Rejected messages get Undo in the sheet, plus Edit transaction for confirmed ones.

Day groups

Your question on what comes after Today and Yesterday.

Today, Yesterday, then the date

Every earlier day reads as weekday and date: “Thu 19 Nov”, “Mon 16 Nov”. The month selector already limits the list to one month, so no group spans months and no year is needed.

Only days with messages

Days without messages are skipped, so there are no empty headers.

Past months

When you look at October, there is no Today or Yesterday; every header is a date.

Header right side

To review shows how many are left that day. Confirmed and All show that day's money out, counting only your share of splits.

Still opendecide

Should “This week” and “Earlier” buckets replace single days once the month is long? I'd keep single days: a month has at most 31 headers and you remember spends by day.
