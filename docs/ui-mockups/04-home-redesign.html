What changed

  1. Bigger spent figure. The month's spend is the largest thing on screen, with its bar and a "Today" mark.
  2. Pace sentence. One plain line says how far ahead of or behind pace the month is, next to what is left.
  3. Three sub-sections. Avoidable, Cash on hand and Owed to you sit directly under the total.
  4. Category rows. The name stands alone; spent and "left" are stacked on the right. Each bar has the same unlabelled today mark.
  5. Unchanged. Month selector, every category shown, review line, bottom-right + button.
